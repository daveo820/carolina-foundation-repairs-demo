# Design DNA: Carolina Foundation Repairs concept

**Archetype:** an engineering report and survey drawing. Graph paper backgrounds, a title block under the hero photo, a "sheet index" of repairs numbered S-101 to S-107, checkbox field lists, and mono labels.

**Signature:** the laser level line. A red beam with a glowing source dot sweeps across the hero photo, under each page head and over the footer, with a reading such as "LEVEL 0.0°". The kicker mark is a small laser dot and beam.

**Motion:** "level." The red half of the home h1 ("His crew levels it.") starts tilted 4 degrees and settles to level on load (pure CSS, so it never waits on JS). Laser beams sweep in from the source dot when they scroll into view. Reduced motion turns it off.

**Type:** Schibsted Grotesk 800 and 900 for display, tight tracking. Spline Sans for body. Spline Sans Mono for labels, sheet numbers and readouts. None of these have been used in another LuminArch demo.

**Color:** sampled from the CFR logo red (#c3000b) plus charcoal and survey paper.
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| paper | #f3f4f0 | page |
| grid | #dde2e3 | graph lines |
| char | #1f2226 | title block, sheet index, footer |
| red | #c3000b | logo red, CTAs |
| laser | #e3262d | signature beam |
| ink | #16181b | text |
| mute | #565d65 | secondary text |

**Section shapes, home:** type over graph paper; a 21:8 photo with the laser across it; a 4 cell title block (2,000+, 4.5, 2004, P-1776); a field check list; a real before and after of a leaning chimney; a dark sheet index; one big pull quote about lasers and level floors; three reviews; an engineering card next to a pier bracket photo.

**Photos:** all from carolinafoundationrepairs.com (uploaded 2011 and 2012). They are real but old. Placeholders flag where new photos should go.
